## Verified specifications

**U2, English printed page 62:** microwave output **700 W**, grill heater
**800 W**, cavity **20 l**, exterior **450 x 290 x 370 mm**, approximate
uncrated weight **12.5 kg**, and stated power consumption **1080 W**.
Input power, microwave output and grill-heater rating are different figures.
Do not prescribe 800 W of microwave power: the microwave maximum remains
700 W. No convection function is established here.

Do not carry over P1's 245 mm turntable diameter or internal dimensions:
those came from the superseded model's sheet.

## Controls from the corrected manual

**U2, English printed pages 57-59:** a cooking-power/function selector and
a **0-30 minute timer knob**. Opening the door pauses cooking; closing
resumes it. Turning the timer to zero stops cooking. Select the appropriate
microwave power, DEFROST, GRILL or COMB function, then set the timer.
The English instructions do not enumerate intermediate microwave wattages;
use the actual panel and PDF pictograms rather than guessed dial positions.

| Combination | Microwave share of time | Grill share of time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| COMB 1 | 17% | 83% |
| COMB 2 | 28% | 72% |
| COMB 3 | 40% | 60% |

These are the manual's time shares, not independent wattage settings.
For the rice reheating proposal use **microwave-only**, not GRILL/COMB.
U2 says the supplied grill rack is for grill/combination only, not
microwave-only. This is not approval for arbitrary metal cookware.

## Choosing microwave versus air fryer

The following are general method-selection principles, not manufacturer
performance guarantees.

| Goal | Preferred starting approach | Reason / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reheat moist rice, sauced food or a dense leftover portion | Microwave, in smaller shallow portions | Easier to retain moisture and stir; cold spots still need attention |
| Warm soup or a drink | Microwave in a suitable vented container | No need for surface browning; beware very hot liquids and steam |
| Restore a crisp surface or roast nuts | Air fryer with the recorded recipe plan | Circulating hot air browns/dries the outside |
| Defrost | U2's DEFROST instructions, checking actual panel | Uneven thawing/partial cooking requires care; follow food-specific instructions |
| Grill/brown a surface | U2's GRILL or COMB instructions with compatible cookware | Available in corrected manual; no personal trial established |
| Reheat in unidentified glass | First verify container compatibility or transfer food | Fitting inside either appliance is not approval for heating |

A microwave heats food unevenly too: it does not automatically make a
large casserole safe or eliminate the need for stirring and standing.

## Reheating principles and safety

Use known microwave-safe containers and vented microwave-safe covers.
Do not use sealed containers, metal dishes or foil in the proposed
microwave workflow. Do not run empty. Use dry hand protection and open
covers away from the face to avoid steam burns.

Reheat only the portion to be eaten, spread relatively shallowly. For dry
rice, a little water and a vented cover can help retain moisture. Break up
clumps, stir from edges to centre, and check multiple locations. Follow
the appliance/food standing instructions. Heat until steaming hot
throughout; a timer or hot container alone is not proof of the food's
temperature. A probe thermometer improves assessment.

Rice additionally requires safe prior cooling/storage. UK FSA guidance
recommends rapid cooling, ideally within 1 hour, no more than one day
refrigerated before reheating, and only one reheating. Heating cannot
reliably rescue rice stored unsafely. See E1 for the conditional proposed
700 W reheating/check intervals; they are not a tested recipe.

Source: [UK FSA home food fact checker](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker).

## Cleaning and maintenance

U2's English section, printed page 60, says to switch off and unplug before
cleaning, wipe spills with a damp cloth, avoid harsh/abrasive products and
keep water out of ventilation openings and controls. It describes washing
the glass plate and cleaning the roller ring/cavity floor. Confirm
model-specific care instructions before removing parts.

Do not remove the casing or attempt high-voltage repairs. Do not use an
appliance with damaged door/seals; arrange qualified service.
