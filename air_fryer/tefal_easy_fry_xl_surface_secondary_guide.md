## Product Overview

The EASY FRY™ XL SURFACE is an air fryer designed for healthy cooking using hot air circulation. It features multiple automatic cooking modes and a digital touch panel for easy operation.

## Appliance Components

- A. Basket
- B. Basket Handle
- C. Digital Touch Panel
- D. Time/Temperature Display

## Automatic Cooking Modes

- Fries
- Nuggets
- Chicken Drumsticks
- Fish
- Vegetables
- Cookies
- Bacon / Beef Bacon
- Drying
- Frozen Pizza
- Fresh Pizza

## Preparation

### Before First Use

1. Remove all packaging and stickers.
2. Clean the basket with hot water, mild detergent, and a non-abrasive sponge. (Note: Basket is dishwasher safe).
3. Wipe the interior and exterior of the appliance with a damp cloth. Do not fill the basket with oil or frying fat.

### Getting Ready for Use

1. Place the appliance on a flat, stable, heat-resistant surface, away from water.
2. Do not fill the basket with oil or other liquid.
3. Do not place anything on top of the appliance, as it disrupts airflow and affects cooking results.
4. Ensure at least 15 cm of clearance around the appliance for air circulation.

## Using the Appliance

The appliance can prepare a wide range of recipes. Cooking times for main food types are in the "Cooking Guide".

1. Plug the power cord into an electrical outlet.
2. Place food into the basket. Do not fill above the MAX line or exceed maximum quantities specified in the guide.
3. Insert the basket back into the appliance.
4. Select one of the automatic modes or set manually to start cooking.

### Manual Cooking

Press the button to display cooking modes. Select a mode, then adjust time (0-60 minutes) and temperature (40-230°C) using the control buttons. Press Start/Stop to begin. The display shows selected temperature and remaining time. An interior light helps monitor cooking. Shake food halfway through if required. Remove basket when timer sounds. Check food; return if not done. Use tongs to remove food. Caution: Basket and food are very hot after cooking.

### Pizza Mode

Place pizza in the basket. Select FROZEN PIZZA or FRESH PIZZA. Adjust time and temperature as needed. Press Start/Stop to begin baking. Remove basket when timer sounds.

### Drying Mode

Place ingredients in the basket. Set temperature to 70°C. Adjust time (1-12 hours). Press Start/Stop to begin drying. Remove basket when timer sounds. Check food; return if not dry. Use tongs to remove food. Caution: Basket and food are very hot.

## Tips for Best Results

- Smaller food items generally require shorter cooking times.
- Shaking food halfway through cooking improves results and prevents uneven cooking.
- For extra crispy fresh fries, add a small amount of oil and shake to coat evenly (approx. 14 ml recommended). Do not add oil to frozen fries.
- Oven-baked snacks can also be prepared in this appliance.
- Optimal recommended quantity for fries is 700 grams.
- Use ready-made puff or shortcrust pastry for quick filled snacks.
- For baking cakes, quiches, or delicate/filled dishes, use a baking dish placed inside the basket. Silicone, stainless steel, aluminum, or terracotta dishes can be used.
- The appliance can also be used for reheating food. Set temperature to 160°C for up to 10 minutes. Adjust time based on food quantity.

## Preparing Homemade Fries

1. Select recommended potato varieties. Peel and cut into uniform thickness fries.
2. Soak fries in cold water for at least 20 minutes, then drain and dry thoroughly with a clean, absorbent towel. Pat dry with paper towels.
3. Add 1 tablespoon of oil (vegetable, sunflower, or olive) to the dry basket. Add fries and mix until evenly coated.
4. Remove fries from the basket using fingers or kitchen utensil, allowing excess oil to remain in the basket. Place coated fries into the basket. (Note: Do not pour all oil-coated fries at once to avoid excess oil pooling at the bottom).
5. Fry according to the "Cooking Guide" instructions.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

Clean the appliance after each use. The basket has a non-stick coating. Avoid metal utensils or abrasive cleaners.

1. Unplug the appliance and let it cool down.
2. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth.
3. Clean the basket with hot water, mild detergent, and a non-abrasive sponge. Use a degreaser for stubborn residue. (Note: Basket is dishwasher safe).
4. For stubborn food residue at the bottom of the basket, fill with hot water and detergent, let soak for 10 minutes, then rinse and dry.
5. Clean the heating element with a dry cleaning brush.
6. Do not immerse the appliance in water or other liquids.

## Storage

1. Unplug the appliance and let it cool completely.
2. Ensure all parts are clean and dry before storing.
