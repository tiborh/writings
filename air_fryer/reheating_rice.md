## Decision

The Tefal can reheat food in principle, but **do not use the unidentified
fridge-cold glass casserole in the air fryer without its manufacturer's
approval for that use and temperature transition**. Conventional-oven use,
appearance, the name "Jena" and a comfortable fit do not establish this.
The Tefal manual mentions silicone, stainless steel, aluminium and terracotta
oven dishes, not blanket approval of glass.

Some glass manufacturers explicitly prohibit air-fryer use: the linked
Pyrex US instructions do so. This is evidence that approval matters, not
identification of this dish as Pyrex or a universal rule for all glass.
Do not try to overcome unknown glass restrictions with a cold start,
lower temperature, or leaving the rice out to warm.

A deep, dense rice portion also heats slowly in hot air and can dry on top
before the centre is hot. Tefal's generic 160 C / up-to-10-minute reheating
suggestion is not a validated finish time for this casserole.

## Rice safety before selecting a reheating method

UK FSA guidance: cool rice promptly, ideally within 1 hour; refrigerate
for no more than one day before reheating; reheat only once and until
steaming hot throughout. At about 24 hours, use promptly if it was cooled
and stored correctly, rather than extending storage. A 3 C fridge setting
does not establish how quickly the middle of a heavy dish cooled.

If rice sat out for hours or safe cooling/storage cannot be established,
the prudent choice is to discard it. Reheating cannot reliably rescue
poorly stored rice because some Bacillus cereus toxins resist heat.
Smell and appearance do not establish safety.

## Proposed alternative: microwave in smaller portions

Conditional on safe storage: transfer only the amount to be eaten to a
known microwave-safe, shallow container. Do not assume the unknown glass
or its lid is microwave-safe either. Keep unreheated portions refrigerated.

Use microwave-only mode, not grill/combination. For an exploratory
250-350 g portion, add about 1 tbsp water if dry, cover with a vented
microwave-safe cover, and start with 2 minutes at the 700 W/high microwave
setting, once identified on the panel. This is an initial check interval,
not a guaranteed finish.
The original Gorenje link and product documents were subsequently
identified by the user as wrong-model references. The corrected
**MO 4250 CLI** manual confirms **700 W microwave output** and a separate
**800 W grill**. Use microwave-only at the 700 W/high setting for this
proposed initial check interval, not GRILL or COMB. Exact dial markings
should be checked against the actual panel and PDF.

Stir thoroughly, breaking up clumps and moving the centre to the edges.
Continue in 1-minute intervals, stirring and checking, until steaming hot
throughout with no cool centre or cold clumps. Follow the microwave's
standing instructions; absent a specified value, allow 1-2 minutes covered,
stir and check again. Eat promptly. Continuous heating/check intervals in
one session are not separate reheating cycles; do not cool and reheat the
same portion again. Do not taste lukewarm rice as a safety test.

Without a probe, steaming throughout after thorough stirring is the
practical FSA check, not a measurement of internal temperature. A probe
would improve confidence, particularly for dense food or meat-containing
rice dishes.

Baking paper is not a casserole substitute for this experiment: it does
not retain moisture like a dish, can leak, and loose paper can lift towards
the heater. Prefer the microwave rather than improvising a paper container.
