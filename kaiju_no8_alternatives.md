# Alternative Story Lines to Kaiju No. 8

(Original Japanese title: 怪獣8号, reading: かいじゅうはちごう)

The title breaks down as:

- 怪獣 (かいじゅう, "kaijū") — "monster" / "strange beast"; the giant
  creatures the series is named after.
- 8 (はち, "hachi") — the numeral "eight".
- 号 (ごう, "gō") — "number" / "issue", used here as a designation,
  i.e. "No. 8".

The official title uses the Arabic numeral 8 (怪獣8号). Written with
the more traditional Japanese numeral, it would appear as 怪獣八号
(where 八 = はち, "eight"). Both read identically as かいじゅうはちごう.

Caveat: This writing is based on the first two episodes of the
series. So, in light of further eps, it may need to be revised or
supplemented.

### A Note on Terminology

To stay consistent with the source material, this writing uses the
following terms:

- "kaiju cleanup" / "dismantling kaiju remains" for the work of
  breaking down kaiju corpses (in the series this is done by
  professional kaiju cleaning companies, e.g. Monster Sweeper Inc.).
- "cleanup companies" for the private firms that carry out this work,
  and "cleaners" for the people employed by them.
- "Defence Force" for the military-style organisation that fights
  kaiju (a fighting body, not a civilian agency).
- "government agencies" for the civilian administrative bodies that
  regulate and subsidise the cleanup companies.

British English spelling is used throughout (e.g. "defence",
"realised", "centre").

## A Problem with the Original

It looks as if the writer of the original story line realised by the
end of ep 1 that the main character's situation is utterly hopeless
without a deus ex machina, i.e. the kaiju symbiosis.

## A More Interesting Alternative: A Subtler Modification

1. As they dismantle large kaiju remains, they sometimes need to fight
   off small parasites.
2. It turns out that some of the parasites can attack humans, so the
   cleaners are prepared to prevent or cure such cases.
3. Eventually the newbie gets infected with a symbiote, which
   gradually transforms his body into a hybrid human/kaiju body.
4. The cleanup company is prepared for this, too. They have their own
   healing centre for such cases.
5. A rehabilitation centre follows the healing centre, where the
   newbie learns that the symbiote will live together with him, and
   the rehabilitation centre aims at:
   1. Coming to terms with it (psychological part).
   2. Learning to communicate with it efficiently (half meditation,
	  half language training).
   3. Learning to fit into human society without freaking people out
	   unnecessarily.
6. So, as it turns out, the cleanup companies are a kind of state
   within the state, having their network of cooperation and bodies of
   cooperation within the government, the defence force, and the kaiju
   hunters.
7. They are not fully trusted by the government agencies for actively
   fighting against kaiju (the cleanup companies are private
   companies, and their health facilities receive government subsidy
   for the essential work they provide to society), but their
   expertise is often used when a new kaiju species comes to be known.
   As it turns out, their expertise in dismantling kaiju remains comes
   in handy for the fighting force, helping them find the kaiju's weak
   points in a fight.
8. Then the story line can gradually merge back to the original one
   when it shows how two cleaners (the newbie and the veteran) start
   fighting for the right to be part of the fighting force. Possible
   themes:
    1. Fight against prejudice.
    2. Learning how to cooperate with "normal" people during critical
       operations.

## Another Interesting Alternative: A Fully New Direction

1. Stick with the cleaner idea.
2. The main character fails the exam again, and the newbie too.
3. There is still hope for the newbie, and the veteran character vows
   to help him have a better chance next year.
4. The theme of expertise in dismantling kaiju remains can be turned
   into a different direction.
5. The fighting force has difficulty coping with new kaiju types.
6. During one of the fights that breaks out near a cleanup site, the
   main character helps the fighting force with advice when he sees how
   much difficulty they have.
7. This gives his childhood friend an idea to propose a cooperation
   plan to her higher-ups.
8. She is part of the study team, where they take part in some
   dismantlings of species they have special difficulties fighting.
9. This is where she learns to appreciate the other type of skills the
   cleaners have.
10. This is where their relationship can restart on another level.
11. The veteran cleaner main character can become a regular advisor
    and coordinator with the fighting force.
12. In return, he can pass on information to the more and more
    experienced former newbie colleague, now friend, to help him pass
    the next exam.
13. The first season can end with the two former childhood friends as
    very good friends or lovers, and with the former newbie character
    passing the exam and getting into the fighting force.
